# Privacy Policy for Video Wallpaper

**Effective Date:** May 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how we manage and treat data in the **Video Wallpaper** mobile application (hereafter referred to as the "App"). We take user privacy extremely seriously. The App is designed as a fully client-side utility to set videos from user storage as an active device wallpaper.

Because the App respects your privacy, it is engineered to function entirely offline, with **ZERO** server connections, **ZERO** tracking, and **ZERO** personal data collection.

---

### 1. No Data Collection and No Data Transmission
The App is built on a "Privacy-by-Design" architecture:
* **Personal Information:** The App **does not** collect, record, require, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) such as names, phone numbers, email addresses, or physical addresses.
* **Video Files:** Any video files you choose, import, or play within the App remain **exclusively** on your device. The App reads files from your device to set them as live wallpaper using local system APIs, but it **never** uploads, shares, or transmits these videos anywhere.
* **Device Identifiers:** We **do not** log, register, or link device-specific identifiers (such as IMEI, Android ID, MAC address, advertising IDs, or IP address).
* **No External Servers:** The App **doesn’t** connect to any telemetry, analytics, profiling, or cloud hosting servers. Everything happens **100% locally** on your smartphone or tablet.

---

### 2. Explanation of Required Permissions
To serve as a functional live wallpaper engine, the App requests specific system permissions. Here is the exact and only purpose of each:

1. **`android.permission.READ_MEDIA_VIDEO` / `android.permission.READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`**:
   Used exclusively to display your video library inside the App, allowing you to choose which video to set as your live wallpaper.
2. **`android.permission.SET_WALLPAPER` & `android.permission.SET_WALLPAPER_HINTS`**:
   Required by the Android operating system to set and fit the live video wallpaper onto your home screen and lock screen.
3. **`android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`**:
   Required to automatically resume playing your custom video wallpaper immediately when your device is restarted, preventing you from having to set it up again.

---

### 3. No Third-party SDKs, Ads, or Tracking
The App does not cooperate with or contain any third-party marketing networks, advertisements, tracking scripts, or software development kits (SDKs) that can profile or track your online or offline behavior. No advertisements are shown inside the App.

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA / GDPR Compliance)
Our App is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Since the App collects zero data (no name, no cookies, no locations, no files, no identifiers), it does not collect any personal information from children under the age of 13, nor does it present any risk to younger audiences.

---

### 5. Compliance with Google Play Policies
We strictly adhere to the Google Play Developer Program Policies, specifically those regarding User Data, Permissions and APIs that Access Sensitive Information, and Scoped Storage restrictions. Access to video files is requested only to fulfill the App’s core function as a wallpaper tool.
